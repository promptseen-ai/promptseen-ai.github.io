## SEO and quality rules
- Target one primary keyword per page; add relevant long-tail terms naturally, avoid keyword stuffing.
- Add related keywords in headings and FAQ only where they fit.
- Use descriptive slugs, unique meta title/description, canonical URL, breadcrumbs, Article schema and internal links.
- Refresh trend and template pages only when links and trend claims are checked.
- Do not publish fake “tested” claims, unsupported virality metrics, or dead template links.
- No About page content or owner/person-related material from competitor.
- No copying competitor article text or distinctive prompt wording. Build original prompt sets and explanations from topic-level research.
