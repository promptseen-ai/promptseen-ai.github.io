How to use these AI photo prompts

  1. Choose a well-lit photo with your face clearly visible.
  2. Upload it to your image-editing AI if you want a photo-based edit.
  3. Copy a prompt below and paste it with the image.
  4. Change one detail at a time—such as the outfit or setting—to fine-tune the output.
  5. Keep the final image natural and check that the face and details look like you.

Tips for better Gemini AI girl photo edits

  • Use a clear reference photo with even light to help the image model preserve facial details.
  • Describe the scene, clothes, lighting and camera framing in simple, specific language.
  • Avoid stacking too many style filters; one clear aesthetic usually looks more believable.
  • For profile pictures, request a head-and-shoulders crop and a clean background.
  • If an edit changes your face, try a simpler scene and explicitly ask to preserve the reference identity.
