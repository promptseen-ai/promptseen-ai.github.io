How to use these prompts

  1. Open Gemini or another AI image editor with reference-photo support.
  2. Upload a clear image of yourself where your face is visible.
  3. Copy one prompt and paste it into the chat or prompt field.
  4. Generate the image and check the face, hands, lighting and clothing.
  5. If the result changes your face too much, try again with a clearer reference and repeat the identity-preservation instruction.

Tips for more natural AI portraits

  • Use a sharp photo with good lighting; avoid extreme filters.
  • Choose one main style per prompt instead of mixing many scenes.
  • Keep outfit and background descriptions simple and specific.
  • For profile photos, use a 1:1 or 4:5 crop and keep the face near the center.
  • Results can differ between tools and generations; review images before sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these prompts in Gemini for free?

You can try prompts in tools that offer image generation or photo editing. Availability, usage limits and features can change by account and region.

Do these prompts work with ChatGPT?

They can be used in image-editing tools that accept an uploaded reference photo and text instructions. The exact controls may differ.

How do I keep my face the same?

Use a clear reference photo and keep the instruction to preserve facial identity. If the face drifts, regenerate with a sharper photo and fewer scene details.

Which ratio is best for Instagram?

A 4:5 portrait is a good choice for feed posts, while 9:16 is suited to stories and reels covers. Square crops can work well for profile pictures.

Can I use these prompts on another person's photo?

Only use images of people who have given permission, especially when creating edits that could be shared publicly.
